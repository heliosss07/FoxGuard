About FoxGuard

A cybersecurity learning site built by students for people who are starting from zero.

Why FoxGuard exists

A lot of cybersecurity material assumes you already know the basics. FoxGuard does not. Each lesson explains one idea in plain language, shows the command or tool, and then tells you why it matters for defending or testing a system.

This is an early project. One module is open right now and the rest are still being written, which is why some cards say "Coming soon".

How a module works

  1. Read the lesson

    Each lesson is short and covers one topic, with examples you can copy.

  2. Try it yourself

    Every lesson ends with a few things to do in your own terminal. Typing the commands is where it sticks.

  3. Take the quiz

    When you finish the lessons, a short quiz checks what you understood and explains every answer.

Progress and privacy

There are no accounts and no server. Your progress is saved in your own browser. That means it stays private, but it also means clearing your browser data or switching devices will reset it.

Ground rules

Only practice on systems you own or have permission to test

The tools you will learn here can cause real damage when pointed at systems that are not yours. Use your own machine, a virtual machine, or a platform made for practice. Testing someone else's system without written permission is illegal in most countries.
